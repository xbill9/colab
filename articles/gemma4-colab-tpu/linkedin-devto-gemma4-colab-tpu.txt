Gemma 4 on a Hosted Colab TPU: The QAT Repack, Served in Two Minutes

One notebook loads the Gemma 4 E2B QAT repack onto a Colab v5e-1, serves it behind an OpenAI-compatible endpoint with a chat page, and steps through thinking, sampling, stop sequences, long prompts and a CPU comparison.

What is in it:

- 🟢 Server and chat page up 117 seconds after Run all
- 🟢 123.7 to 124.6 tokens per second, with thinking on or off
- 🟢 Thinking on uses 857 tokens against 391, returned as reasoning_content
- 🟢 Seeds repeat exactly; top-k 64 costs about 4 percent
